Every argument (spine) screen and every domain (cluster) screen carries one to three figures below its text, each with alt text, a caption that says where to look, a credit, and a `from` label (evidence, illustration or drawn). A screen without a figure carries a `nofig` reason, and the run reports that reason to the user. No figure is decorative, no public illustration is presented as the cited evidence, and the figures are embedded so the file stays offline.
